PaisaPilot – Smart Personal Finance App

Smart Budgeting and Financial Decision Simulator for Gen Z

PaisaPilot is a modern, interactive personal finance web application designed to help Gen Z manage a ₹10,000 budget over 30 days.

Live Demo

"Click Here to Open PaisaPilot" (https://pisapilot-rahavi07.ai.studio)

Features

- 30-Day Budget Management
- Income and Expense Tracking
- Real-Time Balance and Daily Allowance
- Savings Goals Tracking
- Bills and Subscription Management
- What-if Financial Decision Simulator
- Cash Flow Forecasting
- Financial Risk and Health Insights
- Responsive Dashboard

Technology Stack

- React.js
- TypeScript
- CSS
- Google AI Studio (AI-Assisted Development)

Project Objective

To help young users make smarter financial decisions by showing how everyday spending, savings, subscriptions, and unexpected expenses affect their financial health.
